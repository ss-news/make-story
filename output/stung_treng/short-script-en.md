# Short — The Woman with No Legs

Target duration: 50–60 seconds, subject to a timed voice recording. Vertical 9:16.

## Narration

The woman had no legs.

She floated across the yard, pressed her palms together, and sobbed:

“Mother… Father… please help me.”

We were at an abandoned camp in Stung Treng, Cambodia.

Three nights in a row, she returned. Then vanished down the same overgrown path.

Did she want us to follow?

But there were landmines back there. Nobody dared.

Until one afternoon.

Bay suddenly stopped eating, grabbed a machete, and walked straight into the ruins.

We called out. He never answered.

Beside a broken wall, he pointed at the ground.

“Dig here.”

That night, by lamplight, a lock of hair emerged from the soil…

Then Bay asked:

“I was just eating lunch… how did I get here?”
