# Cảnh cần nghe lại tên riêng (chỉ số mới, từ 0)

- Cảnh 10: We were staying in Stung Treng province, along the route toward Siem Pang, not far from the Mekong River.
- Cảnh 19: There were six of us. Me, Bay, Luc, Tung, and two Khmer men named Sophal and Vanna.
- Cảnh 20: They had come from elsewhere to find work too. Of the two, Sophal spoke the most Vietnamese.
- Cảnh 29: Vanna carried them away and threw them out. When he came back, he stood outside, staring into the building for a while before he would go in.
- Cảnh 59: Sophal and Vanna used to stop by for water and gradually got to know them.
- Cảnh 65: Sophal came last. The moment he was through the door, he turned and shone his flashlight back down the road.
- Cảnh 84: The bamboo panel tipped sideways. Sophal sprang up too. All three bolted for the gate without stopping to get their sandals on properly.
- Cảnh 85: Vanna had been lying farther in, nearer the steps. Instead of following them, he scrambled into Mr. Chan's house.
- Cảnh 87: Sophal said he heard something rustling close behind him as he ran. At one point, his flashlight caught a small, pitch-black figure following them.
- Cảnh 93: The next morning, Sophal and I went to Mr. Chan's house. The bamboo panel was still crooked in the yard.
- Cảnh 95: Vanna sat in a corner of the house, his eyes bloodshot after a sleepless night.
- Cảnh 96: Mr. Chan made tea. I asked about what had happened. After Sophal translated, he lowered his eyes to his cup. It took him a long time to begin.
- Cảnh 126: She had an accent from Nghe An, in north-central Vietnam. I recognized the old, familiar way she called for her parents. I could hear her voice catch at the end.
- Cảnh 146: Tung went to get Mr. Chan. Sophal went back for tools. When they returned, Mr. Chan stood beside the cleared ground and watched Bay for a long time.
- Cảnh 155: Vanna went back for a lamp. By the time he returned, the forest was dark.
- Cảnh 161: My hand shook so badly as I held the lamp that Vanna had to steady my wrist.
- Cảnh 191: For the rest of our time in Stung Treng, none of us saw that woman again.
